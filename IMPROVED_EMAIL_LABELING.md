# Improved Email Labeling - Clear Identification

## ✅ **Enhanced Multi-Part Email Labels**

The system now provides **crystal-clear identification** of what's in each email part!

---

## **Email Subject Format**

### **Before** (Generic):
```
Subject: 🛑 EMERGENCY UPDATE #1 - tony 🛑 [Part 1/4]
Subject: 🛑 EMERGENCY UPDATE #1 - tony 🛑 [Part 2/4]
Subject: 🛑 EMERGENCY UPDATE #1 - tony 🛑 [Part 3/4]
Subject: 🛑 EMERGENCY UPDATE #1 - tony 🛑 [Part 4/4]
```
❌ Can't tell what's in each email without opening it

### **After** (Descriptive):
```
Subject: 🛑 EMERGENCY UPDATE #1 - tony 🛑 - Part 1/4: 2 Screenshots + 2 Data Files + 1 Audio
Subject: 🛑 EMERGENCY UPDATE #1 - tony 🛑 - Part 2/4: 1 Video
Subject: 🛑 EMERGENCY UPDATE #1 - tony 🛑 - Part 3/4: 2 Videos
Subject: 🛑 EMERGENCY UPDATE #1 - tony 🛑 - Part 4/4: 2 Videos
```
✅ **Instantly know** what's in each email!

---

## **File Categories**

### **Icons & Labels**:
- 📸 **SCREENSHOTS** - `.png` image files
- 🎥 **VIDEOS** - Screen recordings and camera videos (`.mp4`)
- 🎤 **AUDIO** - Microphone recordings (`.wav`)
- 📊 **DATA FILES** - Activity and telemetry reports (`.pdf`, `.json`)

## **Search & Filter**

### **Find Specific Content**:

**Looking for screenshots?**
```

→ Usually in Part 1 (smallest files first)
```

**Looking for videos?**
```
Search: "Videos" or "Part 2" "Part 3" "Part 4"
→ Usually in later parts (larger files)
```

**Looking for data reports?**
```
Search: "Data Files" or "Part 1"
→ Usually in Part 1 (small files)
```
